Put Tiro on your home screen or taskbar: its own icon, full screen, and storage the browser will not clear out from under it.

about

Tiro is a free, open dictation tool: hold, speak, and the transcript is on your clipboard. There is no account and no server of ours in the middle; your key talks straight to Deepgram. Source on GitHub.
