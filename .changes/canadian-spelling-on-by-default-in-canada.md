**Canadian spelling, switched on by itself if your device says Canada.** Dictate "colour", "centre",
"defence" or "kilometre" and that is what you get, while "organize" and "recognize" are left alone,
because Canadian English takes British `-our` and `-re` with American `-ize`. Postal codes come out
the way Canada Post writes them, `K1A 0B1`, rather than run together.

The point is the default. Other dictation apps have a Canadian setting and never turn it on, so
Canadians type "colour" and get "color" back and have to already know the setting exists. Tiro
looks at what your device says — the language, or a Canadian timezone — and starts there. It is
still a toggle, under **Settings → Spelling**, because plenty of Canadians write to American house
styles for work, and turning it off keeps it off.

The whole list of changes it makes is in Settings, including the words it deliberately leaves alone:
`check` is not always `cheque`, and only the sentence can say which was meant. Nothing is sent
anywhere to do any of this. It happens on your device, in the same fraction of a second whether you
are online or not.
