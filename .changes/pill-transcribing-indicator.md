**The pill no longer says "Transcribing…" while it works.** Let go of the hotkey and the
waveform you were speaking into stays where it is and keeps moving, a swell crossing it
until your text lands, and the pill shrinks to the size of the indicator. It is a word
fewer to read at the one moment you have already looked back at whatever you were dictating
into. The X stays where it was, so a transcript you did not want can still be thrown away
before it arrives.
