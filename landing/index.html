Two things you get back

3× Faster than your hands

Most people type around 40 words a minute and talk around 150. The message that takes four minutes to write takes about ninety seconds to say, and you are looking at the screen the whole time instead of the keyboard.

A thousand words a day is roughly eighteen minutes back, every day. Over a working year that is about seventy-five hours.

46¢ An hour, at cost

You buy the minutes you actually speak, straight from Deepgram, at the price Deepgram charges everyone. No subscription, no seat, no plan to remember to cancel, and no month where you pay for silence.

A new Deepgram account comes with $200 of credit: around 430 hours of dictation before you spend anything of your own.

The honest fine print

  • Your key, your bill. There is no Tiro account and no Tiro server. Audio goes from your machine to Deepgram on your own key, and the bill is theirs to send, not ours.
  • No Electron. The Mac app is native Swift; Windows uses the Edge WebView already on the machine. Small binaries, no bundled browser.
  • Your history stays local. One plain JSONL file you can grep, export, and carry between the Mac, Windows and web versions.
  • Open source, MIT. Every line is on GitHub and the build is one script, so you can compile it yourself rather than trust a download.
Notes for the first launch
Your PC is ARM-based The button now offers the native ARM64 build, with no emulation. The x64 build also works if you prefer it.
Windows 10 You may also need Microsoft's free WebView2 runtime. Tiro checks on first run and offers it if it is missing. Windows 11 already has it.
Windows: SmartScreen Unblocking the ZIP is usually enough. If the dialog still appears, choose More info → Run anyway, or read why it shows up.
Mac: opening an unsigned app System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. On macOS 14 and earlier, right-click the app and choose Open.
